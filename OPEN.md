# Open

1. **Treasury = vault only.** We follow only the USDC in each DAO's treasury vault. MetaDAO's own "Treasury" figure also counts the futarchy AMM and other wallets (e.g. Umbra: $2.4M total vs ~$989K USDC in the vault). The site must say exactly what the figure counts.
2. **68 of the 100 futarchy DAOs are not tied to a known company.** Which project each belongs to is not identified yet (possibly Backable raises or tests).
3. **GitHub links per team.** Not onchain. Of the 37 projects in `registry/projects.json`, 14 have a repository (5 linked from the team's own site or filing, 9 found only through the GitHub API and not confirmed by the team's own page); 10 of those 14 are companies in the table, the other 4 (MetaDAO, Omnipair, Sanctum, Flash.Trade) have no raise we can read. 21 have none that we could find, 1 says its code is private and 1 says it is open without giving a repository. Still open: confirm the 9 API-only repositories on the teams' own sites or X, and the replies from MetaDAO (asked 14 Nov) and Blockworks (asked 15 Nov), or founder claims.
4. **GitHub activity.** The collector reads `/orgs/{org}/repos?type=public` with paging (it used to read `/users/...` and got 11 of the 56 repos of the MetaDAO organisation), and `collector/commits.py` writes `data/commits-monthly.csv` once a day: commits per repository and month since the raise, 721 rows for 80 repositories on 17 Nov. It counts the default branch only, by committer date, and leaves forks out; commits on other branches are not read.
5. **Launch money that does not reach the treasury.** The launch transaction sends part of the raise to other accounts, shown on the site as "not identified yet". Example: Ranger, 400,000 USDC to an account owned by another address, not identified yet. The other 1,600,000 USDC of the launch's non-treasury money went to the DAO's own market as liquidity (the same transaction runs futarchy ProvideLiquidity), so the site does not call it unidentified.
6. **2 of 33 treasuries not read: ACCRUE and TSET.** Their DAOs were liquidated on 12 Nov 2026 and the USDC account changed owner, so it is no longer the DAO vault's. The site says so on both pages. Still open: whether to follow the account to its new owner.
7. **Older launches.** v0.5 (7) and v0.4.1 (6) launch accounts use an older layout and are not decoded.
8. **Admin-key approvals rely on an instruction name.** Some admin-key movements were approved by `AdminApproveMultisigProposal`, which is not in the MetaDAO commit we cite (`74aec758`) nor in any tagged release; it is only in other branches (checked 20 Nov 2026). For those we rely on what the transactions show, not on the code. The Methodology says so (`#unpublished`).
9. **Truncated transaction logs.** The classification reads transaction logs, which RPC providers can truncate (seen on the public RPC). The gate does not check for it yet. The Methodology lists it as a limit.
10. **Team-budget "allowed" uses the launch limit.** In Umbra and Laso the market later voted a higher limit. The page says so, but the number still uses the original limit.
11. **One unclassified movement.** Futardio, 2,976 USDC, class `other_out`.
12. **Schedule.** Scheduled runs start about 8 hours late, and the 17:41 scan on 16 Nov did not run at all. Watch whether each day still gets at least one scan.
13. **Repo size.** About 100–200 KB a day compressed (estimate, to measure after the first week, from 21 Nov).
